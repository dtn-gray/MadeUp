Move the pointer, drag a finger, or use the arrow keys to guide the ASCII skeleton arm. Click, tap, or press Space to bend the index finger. Select the cart when a notification arrives to add a product fragment and crack the screen.

SDG 12 · Responsible Consumption and Production

Made of What I Buy

When buying becomes a way to be seen

UN Sustainable Development Goal 12 seeks to “ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns.” Target 12.5 focuses on preventing and reducing waste, as well as recycling and reuse. United Nations Goal 12

The line from Fight Club, “The things you own end up owning you,” exposes how thin the line is between mastering material possessions and becoming enslaved by them. This idea aligns with Sustainable Development Goal 12, Responsible Consumption and Production, highlighting that overconsumption is often driven by the desire to construct and define our identities. When our identity becomes tied to what we buy, we fall into an endless cycle of consumption to constantly “upgrade” our image, silently draining both global resources and personal freedom. This project explores that hidden reality by showing how material objects gradually expand, take over our space, and consume our true selves.

Notifications get faster as the session continues. Product fragments crowd the ASCII skeleton and the cracks reveal 为物所困, “trapped by possessions.” Reloading generates a new palette, fracture pattern, and arrangement of fragments.

Recognise the pattern. Question the consumption. Redefine your worth beyond material possessions.

Before your next purchase, ask whether something you already own could meet the same need.
